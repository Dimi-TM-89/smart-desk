Smart Desk Wiring

Wire one part at a time, in this order, and run its test from pi/tests/ before you add the next one. Pin numbers below are the physical header pins (1–40), with the GPIO number that pi/config.py uses next to them.

Pin plan changed on 8 October. Our OLED has no CS pin (GND VCC SCL SDA RES DC), so it moved to its own SPI1 bus. Use this page, not the table in the report (section 6.2), until the report is updated.

  • OLED: SCL → pin 40 (GPIO21), SDA → pin 38 (GPIO20). OLED CS is gone.
  • RGB green: GPIO19 → GPIO5 (pin 29).
  • HC-SR04: TRIG GPIO20 → GPIO14 (pin 8), ECHO GPIO21 → GPIO15 (pin 10).
  • GPIO18 and GPIO19 now belong to SPI1: leave them free.

Before you start

Five minutes here saves a Pi.

Rules for every step

  • Change wiring only with the Pi off and unplugged: sudo shutdown -h now, wait for the green LED to stop, then pull the USB-C cable.
  • GPIO pins are 3.3 V only. Never connect 5 V to a GPIO pin. The only 5 V signal (HC-SR04 ECHO) goes through a voltage divider.
  • Every part shares the same GND.
  • Check each wire against the header map before you plug the power back in.

Set up the breadboard rails once

  • Left rail + = 3.3 V from pin 1 (orange wire), left rail − = GND from pin 6 (black).
  • Right rail + = 5 V from pin 2 (red), right rail − = GND from pin 9 (black).
  • Put a piece of tape on the 5 V rail so nobody mixes it up with 3.3 V.
  • On long breadboards the rails are often split halfway (a gap in the red/blue line). Bridge the gap with a short wire if you need both halves.
